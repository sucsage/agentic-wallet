MilestonePay Desktop 0.2.2. FinTechathon 2026 demo: **test money only**.

Update from the app: Settings → App version and updates, or download the installer for your computer.

**New**
- **Act as a group** in the app, like on the website: pick the group in "Acting as" at the top of the menu (or "Act as …" on its card under Groups). The AI chat, Deals, Money and previews then use the group's wallet; deals the AI drafts for the group, and moves on its deals, go to its signers' vote. Each move signed while acting names the group inside the signed block.
- The AI no longer asks to put a seller on the allow transfer list when it drafts for a group (the signers' vote approves the seller), on the website too.
