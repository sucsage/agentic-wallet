Kravii 0.7.0. FinTechathon 2026 demo: **test money only**.

**Updates without an installer.** From this version on, "Update now" in Settings downloads the app itself (checked against its SHA-256), swaps it in place and reopens. No Setup.exe or disk image runs. The update to 0.7.0 itself still uses the installer once.

**Fixes from the team's list**
- An older copy still running (in the tray, or 0.4.x installed elsewhere) no longer keeps a new install stuck on the old version: the new version asks it to quit. On Windows the installer also stops old copies before it copies files.
- AI chat: the "a few more details" form now shows in the app, and so do the "open the deal" buttons and group payments. A preview you confirmed or discarded no longer shows Confirm again.
- Split payment: the preview shows the parts, with "Pay part N" and "Lock the deal now". The seller gets the deal when the last part is paid. A deposit and a split payment work together.
- The AI can find and add MCP servers for you ("install the DeepWiki MCP server"). Adding always asks you first.
- Deals page: what needs you comes first (work to approve, offers to accept, work to deliver), and finished deals go last. Paid, refunded or cancelled deals no longer say "in progress".
- Money: each row says who the money came from or went to, and which deal it was for.
- Groups: each vote shows in its group with what it pays, to whom, who proposed it and who voted, plus Approve and Reject buttons. The owner is marked 👑. A purchase preview can be put to a group's vote.
- Delivering: the Deliver button opens the file picker at once. "Files of the deal" upload shows only for the side that made the offer.
- Delivered files: no-preview file types now say so. A web page can be tried, and the buyer can download once paid.
- Deal and money screens refresh by themselves every 30 seconds. Settings checks for updates when you open it.
- The private link after Confirm has Copy buttons.
