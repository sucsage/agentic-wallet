MilestonePay Desktop 0.2.1. FinTechathon 2026 demo: **test money only**.

Update from the app: Settings → App version and updates (0.2.0 and later), or download the installer for your computer.

**New**
- Group wallets in the app (Groups): the owner opens the group's shared wallet; see its balance and account number; any member adds money from their own wallet; a signer proposes paying members, and it is paid when enough signers approve (each vote is a block you sign).
- Adding money to a group again with the same request no longer reports "Not enough money": it answers with the move already made.
