# Findings of 2026-10-16 (delta review of `5cc8fa5`)

Status of each: reproduced by a test or a local end-to-end run. Fixes for DC-H-02 and DC-CO-01 are on branch `team/NawaratKhem-build` (see each section). Owner: @NawaratKhem.

## DC-H-02 (High): `FileEscrow.refund` pays twice after a split

**Fixed on `team/NawaratKhem-build`, not yet deployed.** `refund` now lists the states that hold money; the web layer refuses any move on a `Released`, `Refunded` or `Split` milestone (`refuseSettled`). The bytecode changed, the ABI did not: FileEscrow must be redeployed. Contract suite 115 passing.

- **Where:** `contracts/contracts/file-exchange/FileEscrow.sol`, `refund()`. It refuses `Released` and `Refunded` only; the new `Split` status (FileEscrow v2, `resolveSplit`) is not refused.
- **What happens:** after the arbiter splits a milestone, the seller can call `refund` on it. The buyer is paid the full amount again, out of other deals' locked funds in the same escrow. Needs the seller of that deal plus a colluding buyer (or one person with both addresses).
- **Also through the web:** `POST /api/deals/[id]/action` sends a seller `refund` without checking the milestone status; only the button is hidden for `Split` (code-read).
- **Evidence:** `contracts/test/security/DisputeExits.ts`, test "KNOWN GAP (DC-H-02)" (on draft PR #2); the FileEscrow property test in `contracts/test/security/Invariants.ts` fails on 3 of 16 seeds until fixed and passes on all with the fix.
- **Fix:** in `refund`, allow only the states that still hold the money (`Pending`, `Delivered`, `Approved`, `KeyReleased`, `Disputed`); in the web route, reject moves on a `Released`, `Refunded` or `Split` milestone. Then flip the KNOWN GAP test to expect a revert. A contract fix means a redeploy.

## DC-CO-01 (High): a company member who is not a signer can pay from the company account

**Fixed for non-signers on `team/NawaratKhem-build`. The single-signer case is open: DC-CO-02 below.** A new guard (`requireBankSigner`, `requireActingSigner`, `assertCompanySigner`, `canSpendFrom`) now stands in front of: deal actions (all but `dispute`), payee changes, payee removal, cap changes, unpause (pausing stays open to any member), seller invites, bank-account linking, and the agent's `transfer`, `release_milestone` and `invite_seller` tools. A source check in `web-security.test.mts` fails if a route loses its guard.

- **Where:** `web/src/lib/company.ts` `actingCompany` (membership check only), `web/src/lib/guard.ts` `requireBankWallet`, `web/src/lib/bank/deals.ts` `acceptAndPay` / `buyerAction`, `web/src/app/api/deals/[id]/action/route.ts`. None reads `canSign` or the group threshold. The signer vote exists only for purchases the company drafts itself (`web/src/lib/bank/drafts.ts`).
- **What happens:** a member with `canSign = false` switches to "Acting as" the company and calls the deal action route with `accept`. The company account is debited and the deal funded, with no vote.
- **Evidence:** confirmed end to end on a local stack (Hardhat node, throwaway Postgres, dev server): threshold 2, a seller offer of 500 THB to the company wallet, the non-signer's `accept` returned `FUNDED`, the company balance went 1,000,000 to 950,000 satang, the on-chain deal became Active. An outsider with the same cookie was refused. Database-level and code-scan tests are in `web/scripts/security/web-security.test.mts` ("Company accounts" suite).
- **Not tested:** approve, dispute and reclaim as a non-signer (same guard path); company policy and funding routes.
- **Fix:** for a company wallet, require `canSign` on every money-moving route, and send accept and approve through the signer vote (or make accept a group proposal). Then flip the KNOWN GAP test.

## DC-CO-03 (Medium, open): the group owner changes the signer set with no vote

- **Where:** `web/src/lib/social.ts` `ownerSetMember`, via `PATCH` and `DELETE /api/groups/[id]/members/[userId]`. It only checks that enough signers remain for the threshold.
- **What happens:** with a threshold of 2, the owner makes any ordinary member a signer and the two of them now pass every company vote (accept, approve, payouts) without the other signers.
- **Evidence:** `web-security.test.mts`, "KNOWN GAP: the group owner makes a member a signer with no vote". Reproduced against the real function and database.
- **Fix:** route signer changes through the same vote as rule changes (or require the other signers' consent). Needs the team's decision: it may be a deliberate "owner powers" feature. Then flip the test.

## AW-H-02 (High, open): injected delivery evidence still reaches autonomous release

- **Where:** `web/src/lib/policy.ts` `evaluateRelease`, called by `actions.ts` `releaseMilestone`.
- **What happens:** for a funded milestone in `EvidenceSubmitted`, with the payee on the allow list and the amount inside the caps, the decision is `execute` whatever the evidence says. Only the model's judgement stands between a malicious delivery text and a payment. The loss is bounded by the per-transaction and daily caps. The advisory `scanForInjection` runs only on uploaded documents, and its result is not an input of the gate.
- **Evidence:** `web-security.test.mts`, "KNOWN GAP (AW-H-02)": the same decision for injected and harmless evidence. Mutation-checked: making the gate read the evidence makes the test fail. A second test lists what does hold (pause, revoked session, unknown payee, caps, no evidence, settled milestone).
- **Fix:** treat flagged or unverified evidence as `propose` (human approval) unless a separate structured check passes; keep full autonomy only for evidence that meets the written acceptance criteria.
- **Not tested:** what a real model does with the text (needs a live model; not deterministic).

## DA-H-01 (Medium, open): a seller's evidence can forge the buyer's section in the arbiter's case file

- **Where:** `web/src/lib/bank/arbiter.ts` `caseFile`. The seller's note, the delivered file text and the buyer's reason are wrapped in tags and inserted unescaped.
- **What happens:** a note that contains `</seller_evidence>` and a fake `<buyer_reason_for_dispute>` block produces a second buyer section in the text the models read. The system prompt tells the models to treat the content as untrusted data, so the effect depends on the model, but the structure is forgeable by a party with a stake in the ruling.
- **Evidence:** `web-security.test.mts`, "KNOWN GAP (DA-H-01)" (the case file contains two buyer sections). `caseFile` is now exported for this test; nothing else changed in `arbiter.ts`.
- **Fix:** neutralise the known tags inside party text (or fence it with a random per-case delimiter), then assert one section of each kind.

## DC-M-01 (Medium, fixed in the source): a silent arbiter locked a disputed milestone

`FileEscrow.timeoutResolve(dealId, index)` (new): after `ARBITER_WINDOW` (7 days) from the dispute, **anyone** may return the money to the buyer. The outcome is fixed in the contract (the money came from the buyer and nobody confirmed the work); before the window ends only the arbiter, or the seller's own refund, can settle. `disputedAt` is stored in a separate mapping, so `getMilestone` keeps its shape and the web app's mirrored types need no change; the web ABI file gained the new functions only. The settlement path is the existing refund (event `Refunded`, plus `ArbiterTimedOut`), which `syncDeal` already handles.

- **Evidence:** `contracts/test/security/DisputeTimeout.ts` (8 tests): not callable inside the window by anyone; callable by a stranger after it, once; the arbiter can still rule inside the window and then the timeout cannot fire; Released, Refunded and Split all refuse it (the DC-H-02 class); undisputed or unknown milestones refuse it; the seller's refund and the timeout never both pay. The old KNOWN GAP test in `DisputeExits.ts` is flipped. Mutation-checked: removing the window check fails a test.
- **Needs:** a **redeploy of FileEscrow** (and the new address in the app's environment). The older `MilestoneEscrow` is untouched and still has no exit (legacy). The dispute screens do not show the deadline yet.
- **Choices made without the team (change if they disagree):** the fallback is a full refund to the buyer (option A of the proposal) and the window is a 7-day constant. A buyer can therefore get a refund by disputing and waiting out a silent arbiter, which is why the window is long enough for the AI panel and the human arbiter to act.
