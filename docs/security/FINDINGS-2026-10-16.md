## DC-H-02 (High): `FileEscrow.refund` pays twice after a split

- **Where:** `contracts/contracts/file-exchange/FileEscrow.sol`, `refund()`. It refuses `Released` and `Refunded` only; the new `Split` status (FileEscrow v2, `resolveSplit`) is not refused.
- **What happens:** after the arbiter splits a milestone, the seller can call `refund` on it. The buyer is paid the full amount again, out of other deals' locked funds in the same escrow. Needs the seller of that deal plus a colluding buyer (or one person with both addresses).
- **Also through the web:** `POST /api/deals/[id]/action` sends a seller `refund` without checking the milestone status; only the button is hidden for `Split` (code-read).
- **Evidence:** `contracts/test/security/DisputeExits.ts`, test "KNOWN GAP (DC-H-02)" (on draft PR #2); the FileEscrow property test in `contracts/test/security/Invariants.ts` fails on 3 of 16 seeds until fixed and passes on all with the fix.
- **Fix:** in `refund`, allow only the states that still hold the money (`Pending`, `Delivered`, `Approved`, `KeyReleased`, `Disputed`); in the web route, reject moves on a `Released`, `Refunded` or `Split` milestone. Then flip the KNOWN GAP test to expect a revert. A contract fix means a redeploy.

## DC-CO-01 (High): a company member who is not a signer can pay from the company account

- **Where:** `web/src/lib/company.ts` `actingCompany` (membership check only), `web/src/lib/guard.ts` `requireBankWallet`, `web/src/lib/bank/deals.ts` `acceptAndPay` / `buyerAction`, `web/src/app/api/deals/[id]/action/route.ts`. None reads `canSign` or the group threshold. The signer vote exists only for purchases the company drafts itself (`web/src/lib/bank/drafts.ts`).
- **What happens:** a member with `canSign = false` switches to "Acting as" the company and calls the deal action route with `accept`. The company account is debited and the deal funded, with no vote.
- **Evidence:** confirmed end to end on a local stack (Hardhat node, throwaway Postgres, dev server): threshold 2, a seller offer of 500 THB to the company wallet, the non-signer's `accept` returned `FUNDED`, the company balance went 1,000,000 to 950,000 satang, the on-chain deal became Active. An outsider with the same cookie was refused. Database-level and code-scan tests are in `web/scripts/security/web-security.test.mts` ("Company accounts" suite).
- **Not tested:** approve, dispute and reclaim as a non-signer (same guard path); company policy and funding routes.
- **Fix:** for a company wallet, require `canSign` on every money-moving route, and send accept and approve through the signer vote (or make accept a group proposal). Then flip the KNOWN GAP test.
